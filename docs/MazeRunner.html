    HOW TO PLAY

    OBJECTIVE

    Eat every pellet to clear the maze. Clear it to advance — the hunters get faster each level.

    MOVE

    Press and hold anywhere — a stick appears under your thumb. Push forward to roll down the corridor, tap left/right to turn at junctions, and pull back to reverse (works even in dead ends). Steering is relative to your view, and you keep rolling until you hit a wall or steer.

    THE FOUR HUNTERS

    RUSH charges straight at you.
    AMBUSH aims ahead to cut you off.
    FLANK teams with RUSH to trap you.
    DRIFT chases from afar but shies away up close.
    They swing between scattering to their corners and hunting you in waves.

    TRACKING

    Colored arrows point to each hunter — closer = bigger. They glow through walls when near, so watch blind corners. A red haze creeps in from the direction of the nearest threat.

    POWER ORBS

    Eat a gold orb to turn the hunters blue and edible for a short window. Catch them for big points — their eyes scurry back to the pen to revive.

    PELLET RADAR

    Catching a blue hunter reveals all remaining pellets on the minimap. Chain catches on one orb to stack the time: +3s, +5s, +7s, +10s.

    COLORS

    The maze is split into six colored regions. Pellets, walls, and the top bar all share these colors, so you can tell at a glance which area still has pellets. Under 10 left, they appear on the minimap too.

    MAP & COMPASS

    The minimap (top-left) is always north-up, marked N. The compass (top-right) shows where north is in your first-person view.

    SCORING

    Pellet 10 · Orb 50 · Hunter 200, doubling with each catch in a combo.

    SPEED & SCORES

    Pick Slow / Medium / Fast on the title screen. High scores are saved on your device, kept separately per speed.
